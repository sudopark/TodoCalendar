This update adds new features.

■ AI Quick Input
· Speak or type and the event is created. "Lunch with Sarah Friday at noon", "move my dentist to next Tuesday" — the way you'd actually say it
· Read from image — photograph a class timetable or an event poster, or pick one from your library, and the text becomes events
· Not just adding: move the time, rename, mark done, delete. Requests that change or remove something show you what will happen and ask first
· Send from Siri, a widget or Control Center without opening the app
· Share text or a photo from another app and it goes to Send to AI
· AI Quick Input is available after you sign in

■ Show on Lock Screen
· An event that's about to start counts down on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
· Add it from the event detail or the day's event list — Google and Apple Calendar events too
· Check a to-do off or stop showing it right from the Lock Screen, and tap through to the event detail

■ Sharing your schedule
· Press and hold a date in the calendar for Share this day, Share this week or Share this month
· In Share Preview, choose which Event Types to include and whether to share as Text or Image
· A single event can be shared on its own

■ Editing Google and Apple Calendar events
· Change and delete events from your connected calendars right in the app, repeat rules included
· Press and hold in the list to delete

■ Plans
· Upgrade Plan lets you choose between Standard and Lifetime, each with more daily credits
· If you run out, Add credits tops you up, and added credits don't expire
