■ Responding to Google Calendar invitations
· Tap "You" in the attendee list of a Google Calendar event and answer Yes, Maybe or No
· The answer goes straight to Google Calendar, and on a repeating event it applies to the whole series

■ Talking to Siri
· Start with a short phrase that just names the app — "Hey To-do Calendar" or "Ask To-do Calendar"
· Not just adding: move the time, rename, mark done or delete, said the same way
