■ Counting D-Day
· Turn on "Count D-Day as D+1" under "Widgets" in Settings, and the D-Day itself counts as D+1, in both the app and widgets
· You can also turn it on for just one D-Day widget when you edit that widget
· Widget settings now live under "Widgets" on the main Settings screen

■ Collapsing the calendar
· Tapping the selected date again to collapse the calendar is now an option you can turn on or off. It's off by default
· Turn on "Auto-collapse calendar" in Settings > Appearance, then tap the selected date once more to collapse the calendar
· Every week of the month stays in view, and only the date cells get smaller. Tap again to expand it
