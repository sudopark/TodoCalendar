■ Customize widgets
· Give each Home Screen widget its own background color and choose what it shows — month name, weekday header, today highlight and more
· Save several styles and pick a different one for each copy of a widget
· Preview every widget in Widget gallery in Settings and go straight to Customize

■ Color themes
· Beyond System, Light and Dark, there are 19 new built-in themes — 11 light and 8 dark
· Pick a background and an accent color in New theme, and the app works out a palette that keeps text easy to read
· Sign in and your themes follow you to your other devices

■ D-Day widget
· Count down to the events that matter, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
· Choose "Add to D-day widget" from an event's menu and it appears at the top of the list when you edit the widget
· Put a photo behind it and the countdown sits on top

■ Two panes on a wider screen
· With iPhone Duo unfolded, the events of the day you picked appear beside the calendar
· Fold it and it's back to one column at once, still on the month and day you were viewing
